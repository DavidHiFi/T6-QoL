Why a donor and not a --load of zm_refreshed's whole mod.ff in build_ff.bat: that file is 23 MB and
   owns ~5,000 assets, hundreds of which are retail names (every stock zombies gun, the perk
   bottles, the Ray Gun). Loading it would put 23 MB of someone else's mod into every build for 121
   assets, and any ordering mistake would hand a stock weapon's contents to their copy - exactly the
   ERROR_CATALOGUE §38 trap. The donor carries only what the four guns need.

   pulled in as camo dependencies) therefore resolve from the retail zones, not from here.
